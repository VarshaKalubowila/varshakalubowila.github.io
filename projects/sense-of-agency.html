01 / QUESTION

Automation can take away the feeling of control

The sense of agency is the experience of controlling your own actions and, through them, events in the world. Heavy automation erodes it. This is linked to the out-of-the-loop problem, to unclear responsibility after an accident, and to lower acceptance of the system.

The project tested whether an AI that shares its intentions, following Pacherie's distal / proximal / motor model, restores agency. It also tested whether EEG can serve as an objective marker of agency, since ratings are biased and binding measures are noisy and interrupt the task.

02 / STUDY DESIGN

One trial, step by step

  1. 1 · INTENT
    Participant picks a target among 4
  2. 2 · AI DRIVES
    X then Y move, 500–800 ms each
  3. 3 · CONFIRM
    Fast key press to validate the joint action
  4. 4 · TONE
    Tone after 350 ms; 500 Hz catch tone on ~10%
  5. 5 · RATE
    Felt control on a 10-point scale
FACTORLEVELS
AI explanation (across blocks)None · Proximal (car movement) · Distal (strategy) · Proximal + distal
Automation (across sessions)Manual motor training with arrow keys · AI-driven supervision
StrategySafe (20% obstacle risk, 2.5 pts) · Fast and greedy (50% risk, 4 pts) in training; risk equalised at 10% in the test phase
MeasuresAgency ratings · reaction times · intentional binding (20% of trials) · ERP sensory attenuation · continuous EEG
03 / MY ROLE

Turning a behavioural task into an EEG study

PARADIGM

Adapted the PsychoPy task for EEG. Built in counterbalanced block orders and interleaved catch trials.

ACQUISITION

Enobio 20 at 500 S/s (DC–125 Hz), synchronised with task events. Wrote the electrode-placement and calibration protocols.

SESSIONS

Recruited and scheduled participants, fitted electrodes and ran the pilot sessions.

DATA

Set up naming, logging and backups. Inspected artefacts and pre-processed the signals. Traced corrupted recordings with the manufacturer.

04 / PILOT & HYPOTHESIS

The EEG set-up works with the task. Next is the main study.

The expected marker is sensory attenuation: a smaller ERP to tones people feel they caused. These responses will be compared with agency ratings across the four explanation levels. The pilot showed the paradigm is feasible. Analysis was still ongoing when the report was submitted.

05 / WHAT I LEARNED
  • Data quality is designed in. Impedance problems, noise and corrupted files from the device needed fixing in real time and a clear troubleshooting protocol.
  • Participants don't always do what the design assumes. Some ignored the AI's explanations. Longer motor training, clearer visual instructions and periodic reminders were my proposed fixes.
  • Experimental control costs time. Counterbalancing, catch trials and binding probes add trials. Separate map sub-matrices per block kept the session length manageable.
